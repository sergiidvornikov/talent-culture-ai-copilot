# Onboarding Guide – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Before your first day
You will receive a welcome email from Talent & Culture five working days before your start date. It contains your building pass instructions, your line manager's name and a link to complete pre-employment checks. Pre-employment checks (right to work, references and a basic criminal record check) must be completed before your start date.

## First week
On day one, report to reception at 9:00 and ask for the Talent & Culture onboarding desk. Your laptop and access card are issued on day one. During the first week you must complete the mandatory e-learning modules: Code of Conduct, Anti-Money Laundering, Information Security and Responsible Use of AI. Your manager will schedule a 30-minute welcome meeting and assign an onboarding buddy.

## Probation period
All new joiners have a six-month probation period. A mid-probation review takes place at month three and a final review at month six. Probation may be extended once by up to three months with Talent & Culture approval.

## IT access and systems
Access to trading and client systems requires completion of the Information Security module and approval from your line manager through the Access Request portal. Standard requests are fulfilled within two working days.
