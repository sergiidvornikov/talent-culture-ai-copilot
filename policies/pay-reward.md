# Pay and Reward Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Pay review cycle
Every employee's pay is reviewed once a year in the Q1 review cycle, at the same time as promotions. Any pay rise is effective from 1 April. There is no contractual entitlement to a pay increase.

## How raises are decided
A pay rise (also called a raise or salary increase) is decided by your line manager and a calibration panel. They consider your annual performance rating, your position in the pay range for your level, and market data. A rating of "meets expectations" or above is normally required for a salary increase. Off-cycle raises are exceptional and need Talent & Culture approval.

## Bonus
The bank may award a discretionary annual bonus. The amount, if any, depends on the performance of the bank, your business unit and you as an individual. The bonus for a calendar year is paid in Q1 of the following year. Employees who join after 30 September are not eligible for that year's bonus. There is no contractual entitlement to a bonus, and employees who have resigned or are under notice on the payment date are not eligible.

## Pay ranges
Each level has a pay range maintained by Talent & Culture. Your salary is set within the range for your level. Moving to a higher level, including by promotion or by applying for an internal vacancy at a higher grade, is the usual way to move into a higher pay range.
