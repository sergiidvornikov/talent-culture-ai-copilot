# Careers and Internal Mobility Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Internal vacancies
All open roles are advertised internally on the careers portal for at least 5 working days before they are advertised externally. Internal candidates who meet the essential criteria are guaranteed an interview. The Internal Mobility tool shows open roles and how closely your skills match each one.

## Applying for an internal role
You can apply for an internal role after 12 months in your current role, or earlier with your line manager's agreement. Tell your line manager before you apply; they cannot block your application. If you are not selected, you will receive feedback within 10 working days.

## Promotion
Promotion to a higher level is decided once a year in the Q1 review cycle. Your line manager nominates you with evidence against the skills and behaviours required at the next level, and a calibration panel reviews every nomination. You can also move up a level at any time by applying for an internal vacancy at a higher grade.

## Career conversations
Every employee is entitled to at least 2 career conversations a year with their line manager, separate from performance reviews. Use them to agree development goals and the skills you need for your next role; the Internal Mobility tool suggests courses that close skill gaps.

## Secondments
Secondments to another team or country lasting 3 to 12 months are available with the approval of both line managers. At the end of a secondment you return to your original role or an equivalent one.
