# Leave Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Annual leave entitlement
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year plus UK public holidays. Entitlement increases by one day after five years of service, up to a maximum of 28 days. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement.

## Booking leave
Leave must be requested through the HR self-service portal and approved by your line manager. Requests of more than five consecutive days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance.

## Carry-over
Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they are forfeited.

## Block leave
Employees in regulated roles must take at least ten consecutive working days of leave each calendar year (block leave), during which they must not access trading systems.

## Sickness absence
If you are unwell, notify your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A self-certification form is required for absences of up to seven calendar days; a fit note from a doctor is required for longer absences.

## Family leave
Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of enhanced maternity or adoption pay at full salary and 6 weeks of enhanced paternity pay at full salary. Shared parental leave is available in line with UK legislation.
