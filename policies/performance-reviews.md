# Performance Review Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Annual performance review
Performance reviews are held each year: every employee has a formal annual performance review in the Q1 review cycle. You and your line manager agree a written assessment against the objectives set at the start of the year and the skills and behaviours for your level. A calibration panel reviews proposed ratings before they are final.

## Mid-year review
A mid-year review takes place in Q3. It is a check-in on progress against your objectives; it does not produce a formal rating. You should leave the mid-year review with any changes to objectives written down.

## Ratings
The four ratings are: exceeds expectations, meets expectations, partially meets expectations, and does not meet expectations. A rating of meets expectations or above is normally required for a pay rise or a promotion nomination. Overdue mandatory training may affect your annual performance rating, as set out in the Learning and Development Policy.

## Objectives
You and your line manager agree written objectives within 4 weeks of the start of the performance year, or within 4 weeks of joining. Objectives should be specific and measurable. They can be updated at the mid-year review.

## Career conversations
The annual and mid-year performance reviews are separate from the career conversations in the Careers and Internal Mobility Policy. You remain entitled to at least 2 career conversations a year, used to discuss your next role and development, not to agree your performance rating.
