# Travel and Expenses Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## General principles
Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business and supported by itemised receipts. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred.

## Travel
Economy class must be used for flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or more; business class requires approval from a Managing Director. Rail travel within the UK is standard class.

## Hotels
Hotel bookings must be made through the corporate travel portal. The nightly limit is £250 in London and £180 elsewhere in the UK, excluding taxes.

## Client entertainment
Client entertainment above £150 per head must be pre-approved by your line manager and recorded in the Gifts and Entertainment register within five working days.

## Meals while travelling
When travelling overnight on business, meals are reimbursed up to £60 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless part of approved client entertainment.
