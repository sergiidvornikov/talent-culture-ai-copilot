# Learning and Development Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Learning budget
Each employee has an individual learning budget of £1,000 per year for courses, conferences and books relevant to their role. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Professional qualifications
The bank sponsors approved professional qualifications such as CFA, CISI and ACCA, covering exam fees and up to five study days per exam. If you leave within 12 months of the bank paying for a qualification, you must repay the fees on a pro-rata basis.

## Mandatory training
Mandatory training modules must be completed by their due date. Overdue mandatory training is reported to your line manager and may affect your annual performance rating.

## AI upskilling
All employees have access to the internal AI Academy, which offers self-paced courses on prompt engineering, responsible AI and building simple automations. Completion of the "Responsible Use of AI" module is required before using generative AI tools with any internal data.
