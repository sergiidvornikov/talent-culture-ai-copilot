# Hybrid Working Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Office attendance
Employees are expected to work from the office at least three days per week. Front-office and trading roles may require attendance four or five days per week, as set by the business head.

## Working from home
On remote days you must work from your registered UK home address. Working from a public place on confidential client matters is not permitted.

## Working abroad
Employees may work remotely from outside the UK for up to 10 working days per calendar year, subject to manager approval and a tax and immigration check by Talent & Culture. Requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Working abroad is not permitted for regulated trading roles.

## Home office equipment
New joiners may claim a one-off home office allowance of £300 for a chair, desk or monitor. Receipts must be submitted through the expenses system within three months of the start date.
