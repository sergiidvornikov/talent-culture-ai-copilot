# Employment Terms Policy – Northbank CIB (fictional)

## Contract types
The standard contract is a permanent employment contract, full-time or part-time. Fixed-term contracts are used for parental leave cover and defined projects. Your contract type, start date and notice period are in your employment contract; if anything here differs from your contract, your contract takes precedence.

## Working hours
Full-time contracted hours are 37.5 hours per week, usually 09:00 to 17:30 with a one-hour break. Part-time hours are a pro-rata share of 37.5. Core hours, when you should be available, are 10:00 to 16:00. Office attendance is set out in the Hybrid Working Policy (at least three days per week for most roles).

## Probation
New joiners serve the probation period set out in the Onboarding Guide: six months, with a review at month three and a final review at month six. It may be extended once by up to three months with Talent & Culture approval. During probation, the shorter notice period below applies.

## Notice periods
During probation, either you or the bank may end employment with 1 week of notice. You must give notice in writing. After probation, the standard notice period is 1 month for Analyst and Associate levels, 3 months for Vice President and Director levels, and 6 months for Managing Director level. Your individual notice period is confirmed in your employment contract.

## Minimum service
The minimum work timeframes that apply during employment are: the six-month probation period; the notice periods above; and 12 months' service in your current role before you can apply for an internal vacancy (or earlier with your line manager's agreement), as set out in the Careers and Internal Mobility Policy.
